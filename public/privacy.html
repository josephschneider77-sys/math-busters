Math Busters Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Math Busters is a free kid-friendly number-block puzzle. It is published by Joe Schneider.

What we collect

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, or precise location.

Game progress (score, high score, remaining hints, and the first-run tutorial flag) is stored only on your device using the browser or app’s local storage. That data stays on the device unless you clear site data or uninstall the app.

Children

Math Busters is designed for families and children practicing multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat, social feed, advertising, or in-app purchases.

Hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard web-server logs (such as IP address and user agent) may be processed by GitHub as described in their privacy policy. The Android Play Store listing, when published, is distributed by Google under Google Play’s policies.

Third-party services

The game does not embed advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. If you open the game through Google Play, Google may process install and crash data under their own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the publisher via the Play Store listing contact email, or open an issue on github.com/josephschneider77-sys/math-busters.
